About Us

From a home kitchen in Islamabad to your family's table

ہر میٹھا لمحہ، محبت سے بنایا گیا

Velours began in 2016 with one oven, a family recipe for chocolate fudge cake and a simple belief: Pakistan deserves world-class desserts made with honest ingredients.

We still bake in small batches every morning. We use imported Belgian couverture, farm butter and fresh milk from farms around the Margalla Hills, and every product is 100% halal. Every cake, brownie and cheesecake is made with classic European pâtisserie technique.

"Mithaas sirf zubaan par nahi, dil par bhi honi chahiye."

2016First cakes baked from a home kitchen in F-10
2019Opened our flagship pâtisserie in Jinnah Super, F-7
2024Began same-day delivery to Rawalpindi, DHA & Bahria Town
